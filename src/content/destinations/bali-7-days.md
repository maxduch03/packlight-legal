I landed in Denpasar on a Tuesday evening in June with an itinerary generated in twenty-eight seconds and one certainty: Bali isn't an island, it's four different trips depending on which coast you drop your bag on. Three weeks in Indonesia later, here's what PackLight got right, and what I fixed while driving.

## What PackLight generated

The starting plan was clean: two nights in Canggu for the arrival and the surf, two in Ubud for the rice terraces and temples, three in Nusa Penida for the cliffs. The Sanur–Penida ferry was already spotted, the transfers grouped so I wouldn't spend my days on the road. A solid base — but three details didn't hold up once I was there.

## What I changed, and why

**1. Nusa Penida: two nights, not three, and no scooter.** You do the west (Kelingking, Broken Beach) in one day, the east (Diamond Beach) in another, and you've seen it all. Above all: there's no Grab or Gojek on Penida. You find a driver right at the port, negotiate both days at once, and forget the scooter on those roads.

**2. Lovina instead of a night in Ubud.** Up north I met Erik, a fisherman who takes his boat out at dawn: €25 for three, so €8 each, and the dolphins offshore without the crowd. Ubud I kept — but as a day trip from Canggu (1h15 drive), Tegallalang before 9am to almost have it to myself.

**3. End in Seminyak, not Canggu.** For the last morning, Seminyak is 20 minutes from the airport: you avoid doing the road again in traffic with your bags.

The final itinerary: Canggu (2 nights, including the Ubud day) → Nusa Penida (2) → Lovina (2) → Seminyak (1). In PackLight, I asked Packy to "replace the 3rd night in Nusa Penida with Lovina" and the app re-slotted the rest.

## Three mistakes I saw people make, and one I made

- Booking Nusa Penida as a day trip: you spend more time on the ferry and in the car than in front of the cliffs.
- Taking the 50-person "dolphin" boats in Lovina: the dolphins flee, and you with them.
- Planning Ubud as an overnight when Canggu is 1h away: you lose half a day to check-in/check-out for nothing.
- Mine: underestimating the traffic. Between 4pm and 7pm, double your travel times in the south, no exceptions.
