Four days is the perfect length for Lisbon: enough for the hills, Belém and a day in Sintra, not enough to get bored. I went with a plan generated in thirty seconds and one picture in mind, the Pena Palace. Here's what the app had planned, and what I changed on the ground.

## What PackLight generated

"Lisbon, 4 days, culture and food." Day 1 Alfama and São Jorge Castle, day 2 Belém (monastery, tower, pastéis), day 3 Sintra with Pena and the Moorish Castle, day 4 Bairro Alto and Chiado before the flight. Each day with its addresses, the hotel placed in the centre, the Sintra train spotted. Clean. Too clean: a plan that doesn't know what time the queues start.

## What I changed, and why

**1. Sintra at opening, Pena first, ticket booked the day before.** The 8:11 train from Rossio, bus 434 or a 20-minute walk, and you're standing in front of Pena while the tour coaches are still on the motorway. By 11am it's an hour-long queue. The Moorish Castle next, then Quinta da Regaleira in late afternoon as it empties out. In that order the day works; in the other, you spend half your time waiting.

**2. Belém in the morning, not all day.** The monastery and the tower take three hours, pastéis included. The app gave them the whole day. I got the afternoon back for LX Factory and the 25 de Abril bridge at sunset, ten minutes away by tram.

**3. Alfama in the evening, not the morning.** In the morning, Alfama is a neighbourhood asleep with guided groups. In the evening it's fado in the alleys and miradouros full of locals. I swapped it with Chiado, livelier during the day.

The final itinerary: D1 Chiado, Baixa, Alfama in the evening → D2 Belém in the morning, LX Factory and the bridge at sunset → D3 Sintra, Pena at opening → D4 Bairro Alto, Príncipe Real, evening flight. In PackLight, one sentence to Packy: "put Belém in the morning and Alfama in the evening", and the plan followed.

## Three mistakes I saw people make, and mine

- Taking tram 28 at noon from Martim Moniz: 45 minutes queuing for a packed tram. Take it at 8am, or walk.
- Doing Sintra without a booked ticket: Pena limits entries by time slot, and the 10am slot sells out the day before.
- Sleeping in Belém or Parque das Nações "because it's cheaper": you lose an hour a day in transport.
- Mine: not anticipating the airport → Airbnb transfer. Arrived late, metro closed, Uber queue, and an hour lost the first evening looking for a building entrance in an Alfama alley. Note the exact route and the door code before you take off: it's silly, and it's exactly the kind of detail a generated plan won't give you.
